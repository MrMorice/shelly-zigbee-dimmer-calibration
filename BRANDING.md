Simplified premium product illustration, slightly isometric but nearly frontal, crisp bold silhouette and restrained shaded surfaces. Add one simple dark charcoal circular calibration dial motif on the face, with one small warm yellow light accent. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no trademark wordmark. Single centered object, generous 12 percent transparent safe margin, readable as a tiny 48 pixel app icon on both light and dark backgrounds. Not a wall switch, not a knob-only dimmer. Deliver one polished PNG asset with genuine alpha transparency.
